Format: Name, Phone, City (email optional)

How it works

  1. Each lead's City is matched to an assigned team member
  2. Leads are split into daily batches (e.g. 100 leads at 10/day = 10 days)
  3. Each batch gets a release date
  4. Team members only see released leads in their portal
  5. Add team members first in Team & Cities
