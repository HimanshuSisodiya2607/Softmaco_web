How distribution works

1. Each lead's City is matched to a team member assigned to that city.

2. Leads for that person are split into daily batches — e.g. 100 leads at 10/day = released across 10 days.

3. Each batch gets a release date. A salesperson only sees leads whose release date has arrived, in their Team Portal.

4. If a city has no assigned salesperson yet, add them first in the "Team & Cities" tab.
